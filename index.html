How Condition-Based Maintenance Works

CBM eliminates guesswork by basing every maintenance decision on real asset data — saving money and preventing failures before they happen.

01
Instrument & Connect
Install vibration sensors, thermocouples, pressure transmitters, and oil samplers on critical assets. Data flows to your SCADA or dedicated CBM platform.
02
Establish Baselines
Define normal operating signatures for each machine. Baselines become the benchmark against which all future measurements are compared.
03
Monitor & Analyse
Continuous or periodic data collection feeds trend analysis and spectrum diagnostics. Our engineers identify patterns that indicate developing faults.
04
Act Before Failure
Automated alerts trigger work orders at the optimal maintenance window — not too early, not too late — maximising asset life and minimising costs.
